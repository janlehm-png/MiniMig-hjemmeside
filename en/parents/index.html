For parents

MiniMig is a creative 3D game for children from age 3, played in the browser. No ads, no account and no analytics tools.

Build a figure together

Choose skin, hair, eyes, mouth, clothes, dresses, shoes, hats, glasses, things and backgrounds – and gear for the games such as a skate helmet, swimwear, a space suit and a ski suit. The figure can turn around, wave, jump, run, cheer and do somersaults. The game remembers the figure on the device. An optional name on the top is also kept on the device only; a made-up name is a good idea.

Dance

Under Dance there are two dance games. In Cypher the figure stands on a street corner with the animals in a ring around it. Your child picks four breakdance moves – toprock, footwork, power move and freeze – and the figure dances the round before one of the animals takes its turn in the ring. In the Dance studio your child puts up to five dances together from four styles – hip hop, show, fun and break – in front of a mirror, and then watches them as a show on a stage with lights and a mirror ball. The music follows the dance moves, and there are three beats to choose from: hip hop, disco and drums.

Little games you can’t lose

Jump the ball – seen from the side, from behind or from the front – skateboard, snowboard, ski jump, treasure hunt, swim world, space trip, hide and seek and penalty kick, each at five levels of difficulty from snail to rocket. Before a game the figure needs the right gear: a helmet and pads for skateboarding, swimwear and goggles in the swim world, a space suit in space and a ski suit for the ski jump. The first time, your child dresses the figure under Gear; after that it changes by itself, and afterwards it gets its own clothes back on. You can’t lose; there are stars and confetti when things go well. The stars turn into play coins in your child’s account, from 1 coin per star at the easiest level to 5 at the hardest. In the Star Shop your child pays with coins of 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20, which is good practice at adding up, and a help button lays out the coins for the youngest. In the account you can see what your child has bought. Special things are for sale here, such as a crown, a princess dress, high heels and a pet – a cat, a dog or a rabbit – that follows the figure, in the games too. Everything else is free. In the browser version, nothing can be bought with real money.

Play that teaches

MiniMig has small bits of learning built into the play. Your child counts and adds up when paying with play coins in the Star Shop, meets letters and words because every button has a short word next to its picture and they can write their own name on the top and hear it read aloud, thinks logically when the figure has to be dressed right for each game, and practises rhythm and order when putting dance moves together in Cypher and the Dance studio. The dance moves have their real names – toprock, footwork, freeze, shuffle and twist.

Brothers and sisters on the same device

Under My figures you will find the button Children and players – for grown-ups (behind a small math problem). Here you add one child at a time: build the child’s figure, write a name if you like, and tap 📷 – the figure is now that child’s own MiniMig. Each child has their own stars and records, and with two or more children the games first ask “Who is playing?” with pictures, so nobody needs to be able to read.

Your child’s own voice

Your child can record “Hi, my name is …” under the Name tab. The first time, the device asks for permission to use the microphone – a grown-up has to say yes. The recording is kept on the device only and is never sent anywhere.

Sound

Under Sound (behind a math problem) you choose which sounds MiniMig may make: music for games and dances, sound effects and the voice saying the name.

Time limit

Under Time you decide how long your child may play each day: hold the button down for 3 seconds, solve the math problem, and choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. When the time is up, MiniMig rests until the next day. You can give 10 more minutes or start the day over.

A good screen for playing

MiniMig works best on an iPad, a tablet or a computer, with plenty of room for both the figure and the choices. The figure is in 3D, so very old devices may be a little slow. On an iPhone, try turning the phone sideways if the screen feels cramped.

On the home screen

Open the game and tap For grown-ups for instructions for iPhone, iPad and Android. You have to confirm the installation yourself.

The first time takes a little longer

The first time you open the game, the figure, all the moves and the games download to the device – about 45 MB – so it takes a little longer than usual. After that, clothes, hair, hats and pets download by themselves in the background, so the game takes up about 160 MB in all. New versions download only what has changed. Wi-Fi is a good idea the first time – otherwise an iPad with a SIM card may use mobile data.

Without internet

Once the figure, the moves and the games have downloaded, the game can be used without internet. Clothes, hair and the rest download by themselves while there is internet. If something is still missing when you are offline, the game shows a message instead of getting stuck.

Privacy

The figure, names, stars and voice recording are not sent to us. A picture of the figure can only leave the game through the device’s own share menu, and only after a grown-up has solved a math problem. Read about local storage and hosting.
